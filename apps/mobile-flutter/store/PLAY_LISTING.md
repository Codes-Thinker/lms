## Full description
Code's Thinker LMS is the official mobile app for Codes Thinker students, interns, and staff.

- Sign in with your institute account
- View courses, batches, tasks, and progress
- Mark attendance and office check-in (staff)
- Track fees and send payment proofs
- Get notifications for tasks, leave, and messages
- Upload a profile photo used across the app and admin portal

Requires an active Codes Thinker LMS account. Not for public self-signup.
